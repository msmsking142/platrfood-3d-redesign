## B. Customer bill (Terms §3, §6 and checkout)

Example bill shown in the app – ₹400 food, 3.5 km:
| Line | Amount |
|---|---|
| Item total | ₹400 |
| GST on food (5%) | ₹20 |
| Delivery fee | ₹37 |
| GST on delivery & fees (18%) | ₹7 (₹6.66 rounded) |
| Platform fee | ₹0 |
| **Total** | **₹464** |

**B1. Food GST 5% under section 9(5).** Website: "Food ordered through platr™ attracts 5% GST without input tax credit (ITC). platr™ collects this tax from the customer and pays it to the government as the e-commerce operator under Section 9(5) of the CGST Act. Restaurants do not charge GST separately on platr™ orders."
Q: Correct for all restaurants (registered and unregistered, composition)? Is the 5% computed on the menu price after restaurant discount?

**B2. No TCS.** Website: "GST TCS under Section 52 is not deducted on restaurant services taxed under Section 9(5)."
Q: Correct?

**B3. Delivery fee GST 18%.** Website: "Delivery and platform fees attract 18% GST."
Q: Is 18% correct for the delivery fee platr charges the customer, or does the 2025 GST Council change on local delivery through ECOs (section 9(5)) apply? If 9(5) applies, what rate and on whom? (The app currently charges 18%.)

**B4. Delivery fee description.** Website: "Delivery fee starts at ₹25 and covers rider pay (it goes to the rider)." The delivery fee equals the rider's pay (₹25 up to 2 km, +₹6 per started km after that, max ₹80).
Q: Is the delivery fee platr's own taxable supply (platr pays rider separately), or should it be treated as collected on behalf of the rider (pure agent)? Does the wording "it goes to the rider" create any tax issue?

**B5. Platform fee.** Website: "The platform fee is currently ₹0 at launch." Q: If a platform fee is added later (e.g. ₹3), is 18% GST correct and which SAC?

**B6. Rounding.** GST is rounded to the nearest whole rupee per line, per order (e.g. ₹7.50 → ₹8; ₹4.50 → ₹5).
Q: Is whole-rupee rounding per line acceptable, or must GST be computed to paise? Any excess collected must be deposited — confirm.

**B7. Customer tax invoice.** Q: Must every order carry a numbered GST tax invoice from platr (as ECO, for food u/s 9(5)) plus a separate invoice for delivery? Required fields, number series, and should the restaurant's name/GSTIN appear? Is a bill of supply needed anywhere?

**B8. "No hidden fees".** Website: "Taxes are shown separately where applicable. There are no hidden fees." Q: Any consumer-law (Legal Metrology / E-commerce Rules) wording issue with showing prices exclusive of GST and adding GST lines?

## F. Restaurant payouts & TDS (Terms §6, §10)

**F1. Income-tax TDS.** Website: "Income-tax TDS under Section 393(1) of the Income-tax Act, 2025 (earlier Section 194-O, from 1 April 2026) is 0.1% of food sales, deducted on the weekly payout. If a restaurant has not given a valid PAN, the rate is 5%. Deducted TDS is creditable in the partner's income-tax return."
Q: Section reference, 0.1% / 5% rates correct? Base: gross food value including or excluding GST, before or after commission? Any threshold exemption (e.g. ₹5 lakh for individuals/HUF with PAN)? TAN, deposit due date, quarterly return form, and certificate to restaurants?

**F2. TDS rounding.** TDS is calculated per weekly batch, rounded to the nearest rupee (e.g. ₹850 sales → ₹0.85 → ₹1). Q: Acceptable?

**F3. Payout formula.** Website: "The payout for an order is its food subtotal minus any restaurant discount, applicable plan commission and commission GST. Applicable TDS is deducted from the weekly payout." Plus approved complaint deductions and delivery-failure compensation.
Q: Is netting commission + GST from the payout acceptable, with the invoice issued separately?

**F4. Collecting on behalf of restaurants.** Website: "platr™ collects Razorpay payments in its merchant account on behalf of restaurants." Q: Is there any issue with this given platr pays the food GST itself under 9(5)? Any RBI payment aggregator concern with holding funds until Friday?

**F5. Delivery-failure compensation.** When no rider is found and food was ready on time, platr pays the restaurant 40% of food value (min ₹30, max ₹300), no commission. Q: Is this compensation subject to GST or TDS? How to record it?
