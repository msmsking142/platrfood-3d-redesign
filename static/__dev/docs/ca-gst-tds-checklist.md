# platr – Questions for CA (GST & TDS)

See also: platr-price-engine-for-ca.md (full pricing sheet).

platr is an online food delivery e-commerce operator in Uttarakhand. Customers pay online only, through Razorpay. Restaurants pay 15% commission on Basic or 8% on Pro; Pro costs ₹2,499 + 18% GST (₹2,949) per 30 days. platr pays individual delivery partners ₹20 base pay, ₹6/km after 2 km, and a ₹5 incentive. The planned customer delivery fee is ₹25 up to 2 km, then ₹31/₹37/₹43/₹49 up to 3/4/5/6 km respectively, then +₹7/km, with a minimum of ₹25 and a maximum of ₹80. The platform fee is ₹0 at launch.
