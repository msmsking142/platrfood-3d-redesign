# Pricing, ledger, and payouts — implementation plan

- **Single pricing source:** `helpers/deliveryPricing` is the pure, tested pricing engine. Use whole ₹ for money, meters for distance, and basis points for rates. `helpers/pricingSettings` loads `app_settings` and any `service_areas` override. The legacy `app_settings.delivery_fee` column is unused.
- **Quote and checkout:** `customer/quote_POST` returns a public quote without internal economics. `place_order_POST` accepts `expectedTotal`; if pricing has changed, return `409 PRICE_CHANGED` with the new quote and require customer re-confirmation. Define `PriceChangedError` in the schema. Store full order price snapshot columns, `pricing_snapshot` JSONB, and `economics_flag` (`delivery_shortfall` or `negative_contribution`).
- **Ledger:** `helpers/financialLedger` writes `ledger_entries` with a unique `idempotency_key` of `order:{id}:<type>`. For non-adjustments, `rider_earnings` is unique on `(order_id, entry_type)`. Hook `markPaid` → `onPaymentCaptured` (gateway fee from webhook `entity.fee` in paise), rider delivery → `onOrderDelivered`, admin cancellation → `onOrderClosed` (full pay after pickup; `riderCancelAfterAcceptPay` after acceptance), and refund → `onRefund`. Keep rider earnings independent of commission and gateway fees.
- **Payouts:** All new orders are online (COD disabled); restaurant_payable covers all delivered orders. Legacy cod rows may exist in types/UI only. `createWeeklyPayouts` also calls `createRiderPayouts`. `helpers/payoutWorkflow` handles `pending` (awaiting approval), `approved`, `processing`, `paid`, `failed`, and `on_hold`. Marking paid requires a manual UTR record and writes a negative payout ledger entry.
- Tax defaults set in DB: food_gst_bps=500, delivery_gst_bps=1800, commission_gst_bps=1800, restaurant_withholding_bps=10, restaurant_no_pan_withholding_bps=500. TDS is no longer per order (quote withholding = 0). `createWeeklyPayouts` computes `tds = payoutTds(foodTotal, PAN valid ? restaurant_withholding_bps (10) : restaurant_no_pan_withholding_bps (500))`, stores `restaurant_payouts.tds_amount`, reduces the payout amount by the delta, and writes an `adjustment` ledger entry keyed `restaurant_payout:{id}:tds:{tds}:{orderCount}`. PAN is stored in `restaurants.pan` and only masked to clients. `admin/finance_GET` returns `taxSummary` and `tdsByRestaurant`.
- Delivery slabs in DB: 2→25, 3→31, 4→37, 5→43, 6→49; perKm beyond 6 km is 6 (founder approved 2026), min 25, max 80. Customer delivery fee equals rider pay (20 base covers first 2 km + 6 per started km after 2 km + 5 bonus) at all distances within the delivery radius. Audit tests: deliveryPricing.spec "production pricing audit". CA doc: static/__dev/docs/platr-price-engine-for-ca.md.
- **Provider readiness:** Automated transfers are not built. `admin/payout_providers_GET` is read-only and checks Razorpay Route and RazorpayX (`RAZORPAYX_ACCOUNT_NUMBER`) readiness; it must not initiate transfers.
- **Schema constraint:** `z.discriminatedUnion` options must be plain `ZodObject` instances; do not call `.refine` on an option object.
