# Food ratings & complaints

- **Tables:** `order_ratings` (one per order; maintain `restaurants.rating_sum` and `rating_count`, including rating edits); `food_complaints` (one per order; private photos at `food-complaints/{userId}/uuid.ext`); `restaurant_adjustments` (negative amounts; `payout_id IS NULL` means open); `restaurant_payouts.adjustment_total`.
- **Settings:** `app_settings.food_complaint_refund_limit` (3), `food_complaint_refund_bps` (5000), `food_complaint_window_hours` (24).
- **Engine:** `helpers/foodComplaints`. Food-only refund is `round(orders.items_total × bps / 10,000)`, capped at the food amount and order total; do not refund taxes or fees. The limit counts `pending` and `approved` complaints. Over-limit submissions become `feedback_only`, with no refund; email customer and restaurant that feedback was received and will be shared with the restaurant.
- **Approval:** In one transaction, mark the complaint approved, create the restaurant adjustment, and write adjustment ledger entry `food_complaint:{id}:restaurant_deduction`. Credit wallet orders immediately with a `complaint_refund` wallet transaction. For UPI, request a Razorpay partial refund with speed `optimum` and notes `purpose=zipto_food_complaint`, `complaintId={id}`. Before retrying, search existing refunds by `notes.complaintId` to prevent duplicates. Write customer-refund ledger entry `food_complaint:{id}:customer_refund` once. A failed refund leaves approval intact, sets `refund_status=failed`, and offers admin **Retry refund**.
- **Webhooks:** Route `refund.processed` and `refund.failed` through `foodComplaints.onRazorpayRefundEvent` first. A partial refund must never mark the whole order refunded.
- **Payouts:** `createWeeklyPayouts` applies open adjustments without making the payout negative, links applied rows via `payout_id`, and carries any remainder forward. Do not add another ledger entry. Weekly payouts still require manual admin approval and UTR.
- **Endpoints to implement:** `customer/rate_order`, `customer/complaint_upload`, `customer/complaint`, `admin/complaints_GET`, `admin/complaint_action`.
- **UI to implement:** `OrderFeedback` on orders; `AdminFoodComplaints` at `#food-complaints`; complaint adjustments in `MerchantFinance` and `MerchantOverview`.
- **Email:** `sendZiptoEmail` with dedupe keys `complaint:{id}:...` (live sender noreply@mail.ziptofood.online). See email-workflows.md.
