The updated design system combines a cool, crisp base with vibrant, high-contrast accents to evoke a clean, modern aesthetic. The background layers rely on cool off-whites (`#FAF8FF`) and soft neutral cool borders (`#E3E6EF`), creating a fresh canvas. Deep black (`#111111`) text ensures excellent legibility, while the brand's signature yellow (`#FFC400`) remains the core primary action color. Teal accents (`#0D9488`, `#E6F6F3`) are introduced for successful and secondary states, providing a calm but distinct alternative to standard grays or blues. Shadows are now soft, diffuse, and slightly cool-tinted (`rgba(19, 27, 46, ...)`), pairing well with slightly more relaxed radii (`8px` to `24px`) to give the interface an approachable, polished feel. Additional glassmorphism properties are defined to allow for modern frosted overlays where appropriate. The unified font remains Inter, establishing a highly legible typographic hierarchy.
